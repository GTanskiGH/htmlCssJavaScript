Background and Origination

Since my sophmore year of high school, I have shared a group chat with some of my friends. In this group chat, we tend to talk on what is going on in our lives, what we should do later in the month, and topics along those lines. However, in order to improve my art skills I have taken upon myself the task of creating one drawing per day, and then I would post it in the group chat to hold myself accountable. Thus, the trend of the Daily Doodles™ has became a poplar tradition amongst my friend group.

Description and Purpose

I do believe that my art skills have definitely improved since I first started them, so I would like to share some of these with the public. This is why this website exists. The gallery you will see will act as a sort of timeline of my art improvement, and I hope you enjoy looking at it as much as I did making it.
