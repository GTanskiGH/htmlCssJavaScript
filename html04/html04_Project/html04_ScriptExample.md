# Lesson 04: Promo Script

## Daily Doodles

Fill this in on paper first. Time it out loud with your partner before you show it to Mr. McMaster.

| Seconds | Who | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Narrator | What happens when an artist is pushed to their limit? | Camera dramatically zooms in on a figure vigorously working on a canvas |
| 5-9 | Artist | Turns out, they improve immensely. | He faces the camera, speaking to the audience as he continues to paint |
| 9–15 | Artist | From poor to perfect, you can watch how a daily drawing habit became a business opportunity for both me AND you. | The artist continues to talk, while the left side of the screen shows two images, the one on top being much worse than the bottom one, and points to the camera at the end. |
| 15-17 | Narrator | I don't believe you. | Camera zooms out, with half of the screen focused on the space above the figure |
| 17-20 | Artist | Why not see the Daily Doodle Collection for yourself? | The link to the site pops up at the top of the screen, with the canvas spinning around to show an arrow pointing to it |

**Word count: 49 words** (aim for about 45)

**Timed reading:** 17 seconds (has to be 17 or under)

### Daily Doodle Shot List

1. Figure paints on canvas, as the narrator remains off camera
2. Figure looks at camera and over the canvas
3. Camera slowly "pans" to the side, as images show up in post
4. Figure points to camera on cue of "...AND you"
5. Camera returns to its state, as narrator questions trust
6. Figure faces camera as he turns the canvas towards the audience, showing an arrow pointing to the link above.
