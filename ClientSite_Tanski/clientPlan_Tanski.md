# Site Plan: Valor Hauling

## The Client

Clients: CJ / Brendan  
Business Name: Valor Hauling  
Purpose: The main purpose of Valor Hauling is to remove junk from homes. They want to stand out from their competitors by offering fast and efficient service while also caring more about the job itself and the connection with their clients than the money involved with it.

## The Goal

When a visitor stumbles upon Valor Hauling, Cj and Brendan want them to have an easy navigation path towards hiring them for whatever job they need, as well as contacting them in order to initiate that process as smooth as possible.  

(call, contact, call to action)

## Pages

Home: An index page that greets the visitor and guides them towards signing up

Contact: The main ways that the visitor can initiate the process of junk removal

Service Showcase: Provides a showcase of some of the services that they offer.

Career Application: If any visitor wants to become an employee of the company, they can go to this tab to fill out a job application.

Terms: Provides further insight into the rules and regulations of what the company can and cannot do.

Sponsors: If any sponsors want to fund Valor Hauling, their name will be listed here.
