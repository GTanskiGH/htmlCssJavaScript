About Me

Hello! I'm Grant Tanski, a junior at Buckeye Schools, and now a first semester student of the Medina County Career Center's Software Engineering program. In my free time, I usually tend to gravitate towards drawing, solving puzzles, and playing video games. I hope this page will allow you to get to know more about me.

My Interests

Art

Although I may not be the best at drawing, I still find enjoyment from it, and I strive to get better at it each day. I have internalized this urge to get better by attempting to finish one drawing each day to put in a group chat with my friends. It has started to become a tradition, and some of my friends have also joined in on posting their own daily doodles. I tend to not restrict myself to any medium, but I mostly do my art digitally.

Puzzles

I am obsessed with puzzles. Whether it comes from video games, movies, or anything else, I love trying to connect the dots and especially the feeling when it all fits together. Back in the eighth grade, when I participated on my school's science olympiad team, I focused most of my efforts on cryptography, as I recognized some of the ciphers used from the puzzles I do. The sheer satisfaction I get from solving a puzzle is a feeling truly unmatched.

Video Games

Ever since I was born, I felt that I had a deep connection wih both video games and the internet as a whole. Instead of pursuing sports as a kid, I was stuck inside scrolling YouTube and playing Roblox games. I remember vividly that my first time being exposed to programming was through Roblox itself. I remember the late nights, sitting at a wobbly grey desk, trying to learn Lua. Although I never made much progress using Roblox, I feel that simply getting my feet wet with computer science concepts eventually led to my interest in game design and development. In fact, I am taking this class at the Career Center to pursue this dream of becoming a game developer.

I will repeat this for each week, incorporating more and more time into the running division. I want to do this because I want to both build up my endurance, and become a healthier individual.

As I said earlier, I want to become a game developer, so I may fill some of my free time by learning the software that cultivates game development.

Fun Fact

Although I already said that I like to solve puzzles, my interest goes far deeper than that. My main interest is solving Rubik's Cubes. I know it sounds nerdy, and it probably is, I find interest in solving them. But my interest does not stem from only solving them; I like to solve them as fast as possible. In the same way as a sprinter wants to lower his 100m time, I want to lower my solving time. It is not only me who has this interest, as there is a whole community dedicated to doing this exact same thing. They host competitions across the globe, some of which that I have attended, and it has started to shape itself into a sport of its own. I do not know exactly why I love speedcubing, but I think it comes from my desire to improve.
