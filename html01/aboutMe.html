About Me

Hello! I'm Grant Tanski, a junior at Buckeye Schools, and now a first semester student of the Medina County Career Center's Software Engineering program. In my free time, I usually tend to gravitate towards drawing, solving puzzles, and playing video games. I hope this page will allow you to get to know more about me.

My Interests

Art

Although I may not be the best at drawing, I still find enjoyment from it, and I strive to get better at it each day. I have internalized this urge to get better by attempting to finish one drawing each day to put in a group chat with my friends. It has started to become a tradition, and some of my friends have also joined in on posting their own daily doodles. I tend to not restrict myself to any medium, but I mostly do my art digitally.
