Featured Businesses

Plenty of local businesses surround themselves around the heart of Medina, the town square. From mouth-watering meals to astounding experiences, the square offers residents and visitors alike with an unforgettable experience. Regarding the former department, one restaurant participates in this casual dining like no other.


P.J. Marley's specializes in food that is sure to make anyone who walks into its welcoming atmosphere a fan. From tasty salads known around the square, to burgers based off of the district's schools, P.J. Marley's is as charming as a local restaurant can get. Set inside a former Key Bank location, it invites its customers to dwell upon its inspiring history while chowing down on an amazing meal.


However, some places try to divert their attention away from the food business and attempt to give the visitors an experience they can never forget,


Castle Noel is a powerhouse of tourism amongst the backdrop of Medina county. It is a museum dedicated to perserving, capturing, and displaying artifacts from the Christmas spirit. Every winter, thousand and thousands of Christmas aficionados take a pilgrimage to the treats it has inside. Castle Noel is the closest Ohio has to a winter wonderland.


Services Directory

Across Medina's rustic landscapes, each building serves its own purpose. No matter what area you come across, the buildings are designed to welcome you to the area, provide you with an incredible experience, or protect you from what may happen.


Restaurants

Around the square, a plethora of restaurants of varying styles make themselves known. From the fine dining found at Thyme², to the Mexican marvels found at Monarca, Medina offers a wide selection of restaurants for its visitors to eat at.


Retail Shops

However, Medina's square is not just a hub for restaurants, as there are plenty of shopping opportunities around its vicinity. The Medina Farmer's Exchange serves as the area's grocery store, with specialty goods and phenomenal ingredients being sold at all times. Besides food, the square sells books in the form of Black Cat Books & Oddities.


Professional Services

Lastly, the square offers services that allow residents different opportunities to support themselves and explore their interests in times of need. For instance, the Medina County Courthouse allows for any legal troubles to be sorted out in a professional manner. In addition, the Towne Square Commons houses plenty of services such as a gallery and a cosmetology area.

Community Events

Throughout the year, the Medina Square tends to host a myriad of events. Some may take place often, while others may only go by annually. However, each even is celebrated to its fullest.


Medina Ice Festival

Taking place between February 20th and Frebruary 23rd, the Medina Ice Festival offers an alliance between almost business on the square. During this event, businesses hire artists to construct ice sculptures based off of their own businesses. Essentially, the square becomes one gigantic gallery uniting every business on the square, and the residents get to vote on which is the best.


Medina Square Farmers Markets

From May 16th to October 10th, the Medina square hosts multiple farmers markets to honor the harvest of its local farmers and entrepreneurs. Just like the Medina Ice Festival, the farmers markets are celebrated as a tradition in the city, and hundred of residents and visitors alike travel towards the heart of the square to help the local businesses thrive.
