Featured Sports

There are plenty of sports for people to participate in. Although there are more than just these few, these sports are the main ones people tend to mention.

Basketball
This sport involves two teams consisting of five people each attempting to throw a ball into a basket to score points.
Soccer
This sport involves two teams consisting of eleven people each attempting to kick a ball into a netted goal to score points.
Tennis
This sport involves two people or two teams of two people each smacking a small ball over a net. The side that forces the opponent into a situation where they cannot hit the ball back scores points.
Swimming
This sport involves individual athletes competing against each other to swim a certain distance as fast as possible, with the fastest winning the match.
Rock Climbing
This sport involves individuals attempting to climb specified rock formations. The hardship comes from either the rate at which it has to be climbed, or the difficulty of the climb itself.
