Card One: Sticky Positioning

Scroll down to see the header stay fixed at the top of your screen. position: sticky toggles between relative and fixed positioning depending on the user's scroll position.

Card Two: The Box Model

Every element in CSS is a rectangular box made of four layers: content, padding, border, and margin.

Card Three: Padding vs Margin

Padding creates space inside the element's border, while Margin creates space outside the border pushing adjacent elements away.

Card Four: Box-Sizing

With box-sizing: border-box, padding and border width are included in the overall width and height calculation, preventing elements from overflowing their containers.

Card Five: Z-Index

The z-index: 10 on the header ensures that as you scroll, these cards pass underneath the sticky header rather than overlapping on top of it.

Card Six: Extra Content

Adding more text and sections allows you to test smooth scrolling and verify how sticky positioning behaves across long pages.
