Grant's Southwestern Stuffed Potatoes Recipe

Description

Personally, I have tried to find a recipe as hearty and comforting as this, but to no avail. This fusion of American cuisine with Mexican delicacies is sure to leave your mouth watering for more. Luckily, this recipe feeds four people, so if the hunger gets to you, you definitely have enough to fulfill it.

Prep time: 5 minutes | Cook time: 10 minutes | Total time: 15 minutes

Nutritional Information

Calories
600
Protein
15g

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 1 cup black beans
  • ¾ cup salsa
  • 1 cup corn
  • ½ cup cheese (preferably cheddar, pepper jack, or mexican blend)

Instructions

  1. Wash and drain the beans
  2. Prepare the potatoes
    • Scrub potatoes well
    • Poke each potato with a fork around two to three times
    • Microwave on high for five minutes, turn potatoes over, and microwave again for another three to five minutes
  3. Mix the ingredients
    • In a bowl, combine beans, salsa, and corn
    • Microwave for 2-3 minutes,while stirring occasionally to even out the heat
  4. Assemble the meal
    • Cut the potatoes in half length-wise and flatten with a fork
    • Divide the mixture of ingredients between the four potato sections
    • Sprinkle it with cheese and serve it warm

Alternatives and Add-ons

If you feel like this recipe is a bit too basic, here are some other ingredients you can add to spice up your meal.

  • Replace black beans with kidney or pinto beans.
  • Ideas for toppings
    • Avocado
    • Hot sauce
    • Sour cream
    • Black olives
    • Green onoin
    • Cooked meat
