Recipe: Brownies

Soft and chewy brownies with chocolate chips

Prep time: 15 minutes
Bake time: 25 minutes
Makes: 8 Brownies

These brownies are best enjoyed warm

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Mix all ingredients
  3. Put in oven
